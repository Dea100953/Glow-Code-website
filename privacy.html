Privacy Policy (Draft)

HerVoice respects your privacy. This is a draft privacy policy for the prototype:

  • Reports and chat messages in the prototype are stored locally (no server) unless backend integration is enabled.
  • When backend storage is enabled, reports will be stored securely and may be anonymized on request.
  • We will provide a takedown process and contact for removal requests. Personal identifying info should not be posted in public chat.
  • Data retention defaults (prototype): chat 90 days, private reports 1 year — configurable by admins.

Full legal policy will be drafted with legal counsel before production launch.
